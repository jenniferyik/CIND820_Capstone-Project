# F&B Menu Engineering and Performance Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This interactive Power BI dashboard transforms F&B sales and cost data into **clear, actionable insights that drive revenue and protect margins**.  
Using **menu engineering principles**, it identifies high- and low-performing items, highlights cost and margin risks, and guides **pricing, promotions, and menu optimization decisions**.  
Designed for fast, data-driven decision-making, the dashboard demonstrates the ability to **turn complex analytics into tangible business outcomes**.

## Business Problem
Restaurants often struggle to balance **menu popularity with profitability**, while hidden cost risks reduce overall margins.  
High-selling items aren’t always the most profitable, and without clear insights, operators risk leaving revenue and margin untapped. This dashboard provides a **structured, actionable view** of performance to solve these challenges.

---

## Project Overview
This business intelligence dashboard supports **menu performance optimization, profitability analysis, and food cost control** in a Food & Beverage (F&B) environment.  
By combining **menu engineering theory with sales and cost analytics**, the dashboard enables stakeholders to make **data-driven decisions** about pricing, promotions, and menu composition. 
Detailed data structure, relationships, and definitions are documented in the **Data Dictionary** located in the `/Documentation` folder.

---

## Menu Engineering Action Framework

| Menu Category | Recommended Actions | Business Impact |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Stars (High Popularity, High Profitability) | Promote & feature on menu; maintain quality; leverage in marketing | Maximize revenue and margins |
| Puzzles (Low Popularity, High Profitability) | Improve visibility; bundle or reprice; train staff to upsell | Increase profitability of hidden high-margin items |
| Plowhorses (High Popularity, Low Profitability) | Monitor costs; slight price adjustments; optimize portions | Protect margins without affecting sales |
| Dogs (Low Popularity, Low Profitability) | Remove, replace, or rework recipes | Reduce low-margin, underperforming items |

## Key Features & Insights

### Executive KPI Overview
- **Total Revenue**  
- **Food Cost %**  
- **Total PPV (Profit per Volume)**  
- **% of Star Items**

Provides a quick snapshot of business health and financial performance.

---

### Menu Engineering Analysis
- **Scatter Plot (Menu Engineering Matrix)** mapping popularity (quantity sold) vs profitability (margin/PPV)  
- Supports actionable decisions such as promotion, repricing, or removal  

#### Methodology
Items are benchmarked against **average unit sales** and **average margin** to determine their category.  
This classification drives **practical recommendations** for menu optimization and cost control.

---

### Cost & Margin Risk Assessment
- Table showing cost risk level, margin, and cost %  
- Highlights items needing pricing adjustments or cost management

---

### Category-Level Performance
- **Bar chart**: Quantity Sold by Category  
- **Line chart**: Margin by Category  
- Compares volume-driven vs margin-driven performance across categories

---

### Dynamic Insight Card
- Shows **top-selling pizza for the selected month**  
- Displays item name, margin, and cost %  
- Provides **instant, actionable insight** without manual analysis

---

### Time Analysis
- **Date slicer** for trends and month-over-month comparisons  
- Enables flexible evaluation across periods
